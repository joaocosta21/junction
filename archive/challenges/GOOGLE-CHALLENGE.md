# Google DeepMind — "Best Use of Gemini API"

Build something extraordinary with Google's Gemini API.

## The ask
**An open innovation challenge — no predefined problems or industries.** An AI agent, a developer tool, a creative application, or something entirely new.

Demonstrate a **meaningful, innovative, and technically impressive use of Gemini**. Show why Gemini matters to the solution and what makes the project stand out.

## What they're looking for
**"Projects that go beyond simply adding an LLM to an application."** Think about how Gemini's capabilities enable something genuinely new or significantly improve an existing solution.

The submission should clearly explain:
- **What problem you're solving and why it matters**
- **How you've integrated the Gemini API**
- **What makes your use of Gemini innovative or technically impressive**
- **Why Gemini is an important part of your solution**

Open to all industries, use cases and creative ideas.

## Resources provided
- **Google AI Studio** — get a Gemini API key and experiment with models
- **Gemini API documentation** — examples and integration guides
- **Gemini API Quickstart**
- **Gemini API credits for up to 400 participating teams.** Eligible teams receive access instructions during the event; keys generated through Google AI Studio.
- **Technical support** at the Google DeepMind booth
- **"Build with Google DeepMind" workshop — Friday**

> **Important:** participation in this challenge and access to Google-provided accounts is **restricted to participants aged 18 or older**.

## Implications for how we build
- **This is a "sidecar" challenge, not a standalone one.** There's no problem statement, no dataset, no rubric — just "use Gemini well." That makes it the one challenge on the list that can be **stacked on top of another entry**: if whatever we build uses Gemini as a core component, we can submit to this and a partner challenge simultaneously. Junction's rules require at least one challenge, not exactly one.
- **The flip side: it will be the most crowded challenge at the event** by a wide margin, since any team using Gemini qualifies. Winning it on its own merits means the Gemini usage itself has to be the story.
- **"Beyond simply adding an LLM" is the whole bar.** A chat wrapper loses. What distinguishes: long-context work, native multimodality (video/audio/image in), structured output, function calling, or thinking budgets — capabilities that are specifically Gemini's rather than generic completion.
- **Credits are capped at 400 teams and distributed during the event** — register early rather than assuming availability.
- The four explanation points are effectively a submission template; answer them literally in the write-up.
