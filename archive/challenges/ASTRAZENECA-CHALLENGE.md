# AstraZeneca — "Equal Care Across Finland"

Help ensure equitable and effective cancer care for everyone in Finland.

> **Key insights session at the AstraZeneca stand: Friday 20:00.**

## The ask
Build an **AI-powered system supporting equal and effective cancer care across Finland**. Ensure time to diagnosis is optimal and patients start treatment within target timeframes, wherever they live.

The solution must:
1. **Identify the gap** — detect differences in access to cancer care or treatment outcomes.
2. **Locate the gap in the care pathway** — show where the difference emerges: detection, referral from primary to specialist care, diagnosis, or treatment initiation.
3. **Recommend what to do** — **"this is the most important part."** Concrete, evidence-based improvement proposals implementable at national or account level. *Diagnosing the problem is not enough.*

## Judging criteria
| Weight | Criterion | Question |
|---|---|---|
| **30%** | **Actionability** | Are the proposals concrete enough for a health centre or wellbeing services county to implement? |
| **25%** | **Insight** | Does it reveal a real, meaningful gap and show where in the pathway it emerges? |
| **20%** | **Data quality & transparency** | Are sources clear, limitations acknowledged, findings free of unsupported assumptions? |
| **15%** | **Execution** | Does it work in a **live demonstration in front of the judges**? |
| **10%** | **Clarity** | Can a decision-maker grasp the key message and proposed action **within two minutes**? |

## Minimum requirements
- **Use reliable evidence** — public data and/or insights from AstraZeneca mentors. **Clearly show the sources supporting every claim.**
- **Address Goal 3** — cover at least one indicator connected to Goal 3 of the national cancer strategy.
- **Provide actionable recommendations** — concrete proposals, not just analysis.
- **Demonstrate a working solution** — a working demo. **Slides alone are not sufficient.**

## Possible solution formats (open)
- An AI analyst that explains indicator trends and proposes actions
- A monitoring dashboard with recommendations
- An early-warning system for emerging delays
- A simulation showing the potential impact of an intervention

## Three known bottlenecks (starting points)
1. **Late detection** — cancer may not be identified early enough.
2. **Primary → specialist care transition** — referrals may be delayed or not progress smoothly.
3. **Diagnosis → treatment time** — patients may not start treatment within recommended target timeframes.

## Note on data
**Data on cancer care in Finland is limited, and addressing this data gap is part of the challenge.** Expect to combine public sources with insights gathered through conversations with their experts.

**Public, aggregate data only. No patient-level or personal data provided or required.**

A strong solution **makes a complex situation clear and actionable for a non-technical decision-maker in under two minutes**.

## Implications for how we build
- **This is a domain-knowledge challenge more than an engineering one.** 75% of the score (actionability, insight, data quality) depends on understanding Finnish cancer care and citing it correctly; only 15% is execution. Mentor interviews are a scoring input, not optional colour.
- **Key resources are in Finnish** (background pack, implementation roadmap). Factor in translation effort, or team Finnish-language capability.
- **Mentor availability is front-loaded** — most experts are only there Friday from 18:00; Saturday has just one. Gather domain insight Friday night or lose the chance.
- **"Addressing the data gap is part of the challenge"** — expect sparse, aggregate regional indicators. Honest treatment of limitations is worth 20%, so overclaiming from thin data is actively penalised.
- Judging is **Sunday 14:00**, i.e. after the Junction submission deadline (10:00) and during peer review (10:30–14:00). Check for a scheduling clash.
- The two-minute comprehension bar means the output should lead with one clear message and recommendation, not a tour of features.
