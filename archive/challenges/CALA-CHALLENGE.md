# Cala — "Make Unicorns Real"

Build a product that solves a real need for founders or investors, powered by reliable public data from Cala.

## Framing
Founders and investors make major decisions on scattered information: dozens of tabs, outdated databases, gut feeling. AI agents could change that, but web data is messy, stale and disconnected. **Cala is the public data layer for AI agents** — an agent-first database of the world built on a knowledge graph where **every data point is sourced and connected**, queryable via **API or MCP**. Covers companies, people, funding rounds, investors, and the connections between them. Enterprise clients already use it in agentic workflows across finance, pharma and legal.

## The ask
Build a product on Cala's data solving a real need for **founders, investors, or both**. Can be focused (a first-time founder raising their first round; a small fund choosing which industries to bet on) or ecosystem-wide (an agent connecting the right founders and investors).

- **Don't try to cover everything** — one need solved in an original, well-executed way beats a platform doing ten things halfway.
- **Slush starts right after the hackathon** — aim for a complete product they could start using the day after.
- **Must rely on what makes Cala different**: structured, sourced data on companies, funding rounds and investors, and the connections between them.

## How they judge (equal quarters)
| Weight | Criterion |
|---|---|
| **25%** | **Real need** — a clear user with a problem worth solving |
| **25%** | **Execution** — it works smoothly, end to end |
| **25%** | **Originality** — a fresh angle, even on a familiar problem |
| **25%** | **Use of Cala** — Cala is **core to the product, not an add-on** |

They explicitly reward sharp ideas done well: a fresh angle on a real need scores higher than a generic version of an idea they've seen many times. **Focus beats breadth.**

## Starting points (not a menu)
- **Raising money smarter** — help founders find investors who actually *lead* rounds for companies like theirs, and who are still actively investing today.
- **Reading a market** — who's competing, who just raised and from whom, how much funding flows into an industry.
- **Investing with confidence** — spot promising young companies early, or get a quick well-sourced background check before investing.
- **Beyond funding** — find first customers among companies that just raised, size a market with real company data, map who might acquire a startup one day.

They'd love to see needs they haven't thought of.

## Implications for how we build
- **"Use of Cala" is 25% on its own** — the product must be impossible, or clearly worse, without the knowledge graph. Lean on the *connections* between entities (investor → rounds led → companies → acquirers), not just record lookup, since that's what plain web search can't do.
- **Originality is another 25%** and they've pre-announced which ideas they've seen too often — the four starting points above are effectively a list of crowded ideas. A fresh angle likely means a need they didn't list.
- **Execution is end-to-end**, and they want something usable the day after. Scope for a complete narrow flow, not a broad prototype.
- **Saturday 11:00 workshop directly covers judging criteria** — high value, attend it.
- Prize structure is the most generous of the briefs collected so far in per-member terms (the Comet programme).
