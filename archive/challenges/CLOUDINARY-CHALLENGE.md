# Cloudinary — "Best Use of Cloudinary" (tech track)

Build an innovative solution using one of their four tools listed at **https://cld.media/hackathons** that uses **images and video** to create a more engaging and intelligent digital experience.

## ⚠️ Separate submission required
**To be considered, you must fill in this form: https://cld.media/hackathon-survey**
Entering the Junction submission alone is **not** enough for this challenge.

## The ask
Images and video are central to how people discover, understand and interact with products and services. Use Cloudinary to build a project that **solves a real problem through visual content**.

The project could **transform, optimize, personalize, organize, or deliver** images and video in a creative way. Think about **who the solution helps** and how visual media makes the experience **faster, smarter, or more engaging**.

**Cloudinary must play a meaningful role in the solution — "not just a CDN."**

You don't need to build a media platform from scratch: Cloudinary handles upload, management, transformation, optimization and delivery. Any programming language or framework is fine.

## How it's judged
- **Originality and usefulness of the idea**
- **Quality of the user experience**
- **How effectively Cloudinary is integrated**
- **Technical execution**
- **Creativity**
- **Potential real-world impact**

## Resources
- **Generous free tier** for APIs and SDKs
- Features: image and video transformations, resizing, cropping, format and quality optimization, responsive delivery, programmatic media management
- **Creative use of AI-powered media capabilities is encouraged** where relevant
- Docs: https://cloudinary.com/documentation
- Hackathon page: https://cloudinary.com/pages/hackathons/
- The four specific tools they want used: https://cld.media/hackathons

## Implications for how we build
- **Sixth stackable award**, but the **only one requiring a separate submission form.** Easy to lose the entry by forgetting it — add it to the Sunday-morning submission checklist.
- **"Not just a CDN" is the explicit disqualifier.** Uploading user images and serving them back scores nothing. It needs transformation, analysis or personalisation doing real work.
- **This is the only tech award that constrains the project's subject matter** — the build has to be visual-media-centric. Unlike Aiven or Context.dev, we can't bolt it onto an arbitrary project. That makes it a poor fit for most of the partner challenges on our list, and a natural one for a few:
  - **Stoa** (construction drawings — image transformation and region extraction on technical drawings)
  - **Hostinger** (visual propaganda / manipulated-image detection)
  - **Aalto** (adaptive visual/light sensory experiences)
- **Their four designated tools need checking at `cld.media/hackathons`** — the brief says "one of our four tools" without naming them in the text, so confirm which before planning around a capability.
- UX quality is an explicit criterion here, more so than in the other tech awards — this one rewards a polished front end rather than just correct integration.
