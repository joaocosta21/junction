# The six example scenarios — what the gateway does for each

Valio Aimo's brief lists six "example scenarios to spark ideas" (`AIMO/CHALLENGE.md`). They are optional. **Two are covered in full (5 for allergens, 6) and four in part.** This page says what holds now for each, at the final code (commit `b567e5f`, Sunday 15 Nov 2026). The proof for each, with the tests, is in [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md), items 7 to 12; the sentences to type and the answers at both clocks are in `docs/DEMO.md`.

Every figure below is from the recordings of Valio Aimo's mock (1,163 products) at the demo clock, Thursday 12 Nov 2026 14:15. None is a figure about the whole catalog of 17,546 products. In all six a person confirms the order: no order is placed without one in the four demo customers.

| 2 | Last-minute event | **In part** | An ingredient that cannot arrive in time is named with the reason. A stand-in is chosen by its role in the dish, says which allergens it adds, and always goes to a person (rule F-09). A date that cannot be met is answered with the earliest date as a labelled alternative that a person accepts (rule F-07). Pressure relaxes no rule: there is no urgency field | The role alternatives are a fixed list per recipe, written by us. No request for "tomorrow" can be filled from the recording: its deliveries are for Monday 16 Nov | Hotel, "100 litres of lactose-free milk by tomorrow": "Alternative: delivered Monday 16 Nov, 3 days later than asked", €248.00. | 3 | Supplier stock-out | **In part** | A product asked for by its id that cannot be supplied in full gets a substitute, ranked by kind, use and formula (ingredient lists and six nutrients). A substitute not judged replaceable always goes to a person (rule F-12), and so does one that costs more than 25% above the product asked for (rule P-05). | 5 | Allergen-safe substitution under pressure | **Works for allergens; in part for nutrition** | An excluded allergen that a product declares can never be ordered (rule F-02). ## Assumptions

- The data is a recording, read at a clock we chose. Valio Aimo said its test data is static.
- The customers, their order histories and their standing orders are synthetic, and say so.
- The recipes, the role alternatives, the tolerances and every limit value are ours and illustrative.
- The full list is in `docs/ASSUMPTIONS.md`; what a pilot would add, and what each addition would stand on, is in [COVERAGE.md](COVERAGE.md), "Next steps".
