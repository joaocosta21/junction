## Build
1. **Products outside the recording.** `ReplayGateway` sets `not_in_recording=True` on the price and availability lines it makes up for an id the capture does not hold (today they read as "not deliverable", which is false). The offer builder excludes such a product with `NOT_IN_RECORDING` and the sentence "Not in the recorded copy of Valio's data used for this demo; a live lookup would answer." — never `NOT_DELIVERABLE`, never in Valio's name. The availability package needs no change if the candidates check the flag first; if it does need one, say so in the hand-back instead of editing it. Test: in replay, a request for butter (no butter in the capture) gives exclusions `NOT_IN_RECORDING` only, and scenarios 1 and 2 are unchanged (their not-deliverable products are in the capture).
2. **The ingredient list counts as a statement (SPEC §6).** The loader keeps `key_ingredients` (English, else Finnish). In `tta.allergens`: a function that finds, in that text, an excluded allergen named in capitals or by its usual words (milk, almond, hazelnut, walnut, cashew, pecan, pistachio, macadamia, nuts, wheat, rye, barley, oats, gluten, egg, soy/soya, fish, crustaceans, mustard, celery, sesame, lupin, molluscs, sulphites — with the GS1 family each belongs to, and Finnish forms where cheap: maito, manteli, pähkinä, vehnä, ruis, ohra, kaura, gluteeni, kananmuna, soija, kala, sinappi, selleri, seesami), and a "may contain …" phrase. When the text names an excluded allergen that the declaration states `FREE_FROM` or does not state, the finding becomes `MAY_CONTAIN` → to a dietitian, with a sentence quoting the ingredient words and the declaration. A declared `CONTAINS` stays a rejection. Words inside other words are not matches ("coconut" is not "nut" unless "nut" stands alone; "buckwheat" is not wheat). Test: `465793` (Alpro Barista Almond drink, `AN` declared FREE_FROM, ingredients "ALMOND (2.5%) … MAY CONTAIN REMAINS FROM NUTS") with `AN` excluded → to a dietitian, never `AUTO`; count over the whole catalog how many products change outcome for an `AM`+`AW` exclusion and for an `AN` exclusion, and report both numbers (no assertion bends to them).
3. **`cream` keeps vegetable-fat blends out.** "10 L cream" picks a vegetable-fat mix today. Add `exclude_terms` for the blends (read the names in category 10401 and say which terms and how many products leave), and a type `vegetable_cream` if the data supports it. Update the counts in `product_types.yaml` and its test.
