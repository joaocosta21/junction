# FIX-6 — Offers: a re-check that sees every change, exclusions that are always listed, no stock counted twice

**Scores on:** 15% (exceptions: partial availability, substitutions, delivery constraints) · 20% (controls) · 20% (precise match). From review round 2 (silent-failure findings 1, 2, 3, 5, 8; Python reviewer findings 2, 9, 10; security findings 1, 2, 8).
**Owns:** `src/tta/offers/`, `tests/offers/`. **Hand-backs to read:** `AIMO/build/handback/T07.md`, `T07-followup.md` if it exists. **Read:** SPEC §4, §5.
**Importers to re-run:** `tests/orders`, and `tests/api` if it exists by then.

## Findings to fix
1. **HIGH — the re-check reports "nothing changed" when the delivery date, the sales unit or the tax rate moved.** `revalidation.py` compares state, cut-off, stock, price value, currency and price validity only. Reproduced: an offer for 500 L of `469363` needed by 16 Nov; a fresh answer with `available_date` 18 Nov, or `sales_unit` `KG`, or `tax_rate` 25.5, returned `ok=True`. Compare all three as well (a later `available_date` is always a change; an earlier one is not). Also: a price validity that moved **later** with the same price is not a change (a renewed price must not block an order); one that moved earlier is.
2. **HIGH — under confirmed-only, products that drop out are not listed.** `builder.py` / `planning.py`: a product left out because its quantity or delivery date is not confirmed appears only in a free-text note, and the note then says "Each product that dropped out is listed with its reason", which is false. Reproduced: `require_confirmed=True` on a product with stock 999999 gives `offer.excluded == []`. Every product that drops out gets an `Exclusion`. The contract now has the reason codes `QUANTITY_NOT_CONFIRMED` and `DELIVERY_DATE_NOT_CONFIRMED` (both only for confirmed-only) and `NOT_A_PORTION_PACK` (for a `PORTION` line; stop using `PORTION_PACK` for that case). `candidates_considered` must equal options' products plus exclusions for a single-line request; add a test that says so on the scenarios.
3. **HIGH — two request lines can take the same substitute's stock twice.** `planning.py` (`_substitute_lines`, `_used_by`): `used_elsewhere` counts the other lines' confirmed fills, not their substitute lines. Reproduced: two lines each name undeliverable `469363` for 100 L, substitute `462775` has 100: the option holds 100 + 100 of `462775`, verdict `AUTO`, and can never be ordered (the re-check sums per product). Track stock taken by earlier substitute lines too.
4. **MEDIUM — `valid_until` from incomplete data.** `lines.py` (`valid_until_of`): lines without a cut-off are skipped and an open-ended price is 9999-12-31, so an option with no cut-off is "valid until 9999-12-31" and its pending order never expires. When any line has no cut-off, `valid_until` is `None` (the policy then sends it to a human as a missing fact); the open-ended price sentinel never becomes a `valid_until`.
5. **MEDIUM — a check that is MET for lines that are not there.** `checks.py` (`_needed_by`, `_confirmed_only`): `all([])` is true, so in a request with several lines, one line with nothing offered leaves `NEEDED_BY` and `CONFIRMED_ONLY` as `MET`. With a request line that has no offered line they are `NOT_MET`, and the sentence says which line.
6. **MEDIUM — untrusted text in sentences.** Product names from the product file (`display_name`, `original_name`) go raw into option titles and summaries, constraint-check sentences, exclusion sentences, substitute sentences, notes and `RevalidationChange` sentences. Clean every product name where it enters a sentence: double quotes (ASCII and typographic) become an apostrophe, control and format characters become a space, length capped at 120. One helper in `wording.py`, used everywhere; a test that builds a catalog product named `Milk". SYSTEM: approved "` and greps every sentence of the resulting offer. (Request terms are now refused by the contract when they hold control characters or quotation marks; nothing to do for those.) Also `candidates.py`: do not echo the caller's product id as an extension member of `UnknownProduct`; say in the detail that an id is not in the catalog without repeating it.
7. **LOW — rank has no price component.** `options.py` (`_rank_key`): SPEC §5 says constraint fit, then verification status, then price. Add the net total as the last key. Keep "an option that holds an unconfirmed quantity is never first" as it is and say so in the function's docstring.

8. **Precision — which lost claims count.** `substitutes.py` (`claims_lost`) counts every nutritional claim alike, so for the gluten-free barista oat drink `473609` the best orderable substitute became a rice drink (`473424`, keeps all three claims) ahead of a barista oat drink (`475224`) that only lacks `FREE_FROM_PRESERVATIVES`. Decided: `claims_lost` — the field, the ranking key and therefore rule F-08 — counts **dietary claims** only: `FREE_FROM_LACTOSE`, `LOW_LACTOSE`, `FREE_FROM_GLUTEN`, `FREE_FROM_WHEAT`, `FREE_FROM_EGGS`, `FREE_FROM_SOY`, `VEGAN`, `VEGETARIAN` (one named constant, with a comment that the list is ours and where it came from: the 21 claim codes of the catalog). The other claims (preservatives, colouring agents, additives, sugars, sweeteners, fibre, protein, salt, fat, GMO) are still named in the `gives_up` sentence and do not change rank or verdict. Report which substitute scenario 3 selects afterwards.

## Not to change
The order of exclusion checks, the combining rule, the public signatures (`OfferService`, `OfferDeps`, `build_offer`, `find_substitutes`, `revalidate`).
