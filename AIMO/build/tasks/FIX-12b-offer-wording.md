## Build
1. **Coverage per line.** A two-line offer (100 L lactose-free milk + 10 kg butter) reads "covers the 2 request lines, the least covered at 0.0%", and P-03 "Only 0% of the requested quantity is covered", while line 1 is covered in full. The option summary and P-03's sentence name each line that falls short ("butter: 0 of 10 kg; milk: 100 of 100 L covered"); the ratio the rule compares stays the lowest line's.
2. **Substitutes judged by the buyer's own rule.** `POST /v1/products/{id}/substitutes` as the ward says "passes: true, orderable: true" for `475224`, which the ward's own offer excludes as `ALLERGEN_NOT_STATED`. Each candidate also carries the outcome of the buyer's exclusion rule (and confirmed-only when the profile sets it): a candidate the buyer's offer would exclude is not `orderable`, with the same reason code and sentence the offer would give; one that would go to a dietitian says so.
3. **The earliest date when every product is late.** When a dated request ("by tomorrow") has nothing that can arrive in time, the offer's notes name the earliest delivery date among the candidates and which products would make it, so the buyer can decide.
4. **The trade-off on the approval card.** The approval card shows, for each line, the allergen findings that sent it to a human and, for a substitute, the `gives_up` sentence and the claims it loses — above the approve and reject buttons, escaped. The approval's reasons already name the rules; this shows the facts behind them.
