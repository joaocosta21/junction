# T04 — questions

## Q1 — for the team: which claims must be confirmed before the agent orders alone?

**Context:** SPEC §4 gives every offer line five claims. Its table sets `delivery_date` to `UNKNOWN` when the request has no `needed_by`, and `origin` to `UNKNOWN` when the product file does not state it. SPEC §7 says an order goes ahead without a human only when "every claim confirmed", and the contract has `OfferLine.all_claims_confirmed` over all five.

**We did:** built `verify` to the table as written, and ran it on tonight's capture (`2026-11-14-demo`, clock pinned to its `timeNow`).

**We got:** a request without a date can never have every claim confirmed, even for a product with confirmed stock and an open cut-off. The same holds for a product with no origin in the file, although the buyer did not ask about origin. Catalog figure: 80 of 17,546 products state no origin (7 with no value, 73 with a placeholder code `zy`, `xs` or `zz` and no numeric list).

**Example to show:** `469363` (384 ST confirmed, cut-off Sunday 15 Nov 17:00, origin `fi`), 384 ST asked:

| `needed_by` | price | quantity | delivery_date | allergens | origin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-11-16 | CONFIRMED | CONFIRMED | CONFIRMED | CONFIRMED | CONFIRMED |
| not given | CONFIRMED | CONFIRMED | **UNKNOWN** — "No needed-by date was given; Valio shows delivery on Monday 16 Nov if ordered by Sunday 15 Nov at 17:00." | CONFIRMED | CONFIRMED |

**Ask:** "Should the policy and `require_confirmed` read 'every claim confirmed' over all five claims, or only over the claims the request depends on (price and quantity always, delivery date when a date was given, allergens when something is excluded)? With all five, an undated request always goes to a human — is that expected, or did we get something wrong?"

**If no answer, we build:** `verify` keeps reporting the table as written (`UNKNOWN` for an undated request, `UNKNOWN` for a missing origin). Which claims gate an order placed by the agent alone is left to the policy (T05) and the offer builder (T07); this hand-back flags it to both.
