# FIX-12a — questions

For Valio. It widens ALMOND-1 in `AIMO/BOOTH-QUESTIONS.md` from one product to the whole catalog.

**Context:** a buyer's agent excludes milk (`AM`) or tree nuts (`AN`). The gateway reads the allergen declaration (`allergen` and `nonAllergen` groups) and, since FIX-12a, also the product's own `keyIngredients`. **We did:** compared the two over the whole catalog (17,546 products): a product counts when its declaration states the allergen FREE_FROM while its ingredient list names it (as a whole word, outside a "free from" wording) or says it "may contain" it. **We got:** 18 products for `AM`+`AW` and 7 for `AN`. Some read as plain data errors, others as a "may contain" the declaration leaves out. We now send each one to a dietitian with a sentence quoting both. **Example to show:** `465038` Jymy Organic ice cream Liquorice: `AM` FREE_FROM, ingredients "WHOLE MILK*, CREAM*, … skimmed MILK POWDER*". `461634` Jalostaja Liver Paste: `AM` FREE_FROM, ingredients "… MILK powder …". `465713` Pipers Crisps: `AM` FREE_FROM, ingredients "LACTOSE (MILK)". `466989` Schlagfix: `AM` FREE_FROM, "May contain traces of milk". `470094` Fazer Gluten-Free Runeberg pastry: `AN` FREE_FROM, ingredients name almond. **Ask:** "When the declaration and the ingredient list disagree, which one should an agent trust? Are these conflicts expected in the hackathon file, or did we read the groups wrong?" **If no answer, we build:** what is built now. Neither source wins: the product goes to a dietitian, who sees both quoted. A declared CONTAINS is still a rejection.
