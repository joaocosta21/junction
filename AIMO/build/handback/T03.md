## Assumptions
- `X99` is a general statement, never a statement about one allergen: `STRICT` → `TO_HUMAN` with a sentence that names `X99`, `DECLARED_ONLY` → passes as unverified (booth Q2). 195 products carry it; for an `AM` exclusion 169 of them get that sentence.
- `X99` cannot be excluded, and a substitute that carries `X99` (`CONTAINS` in the file) does not add an allergen.
- Relation values are read with the GS1 meanings (booth Q2).
- Families: `AW` → `UW GB NR GO GS GK`; `AN` → `SA SH SW SC ST SM SP SR`; `AM` → `ML`; `AF` → `ABD ADQ`. `PN` pine nuts, `SO` coconut, `NM` corn and `UN` shellfish stand alone.
- `ABD` (tuna) under `AF` is our reading; the docs name only `ADQ` as a sub-code of fish.
- Family excluded: a member `CONTAINS` rejects, a member `MAY_CONTAIN` goes to a human; a member stated `FREE_FROM` or `UNDECLARED` says nothing about the family.
- Oats: with `AW` excluded, declared `GO` counts as `MAY_CONTAIN` → `TO_HUMAN` under both strictness values, unless `AW` or another gluten cereal is `CONTAINS` (then `REJECT`). 243 products go to a human this way.
- Member excluded with no statement of its own: a declared family (`CONTAINS` or `MAY_CONTAIN`) is reported as `MAY_CONTAIN` → `TO_HUMAN`. This keeps lactose-free milk from being rejected when lactose `ML` is excluded.
- A family stated `FREE_FROM` or `UNDECLARED` is not read as a statement about a member: 178 products state a family `FREE_FROM` and still declare one of its members (132 `AW`, 45 `AN`, 1 `AM`).
- A statement on the member itself wins over the family statement (477 products declare milk and state lactose `FREE_FROM`).
- Least safe order on conflicts: `CONTAINS`, `MAY_CONTAIN`, `UNDECLARED`, `FREE_FROM`.
- Substitution rule compares codes one by one, without families. Family-aware counting gives 30 of 249 (family on the original covers a member) or 27 (both directions); the second passes `471881` and `464320`, against scenario 3.
- Substitution rule looks at the code, not the level: `MAY_CONTAIN` on the original and `CONTAINS` on the substitute passes. 0 of the 249 listed pairs are in that case.
- An original with no allergen statement and a substitute that declares one fails the rule.
- An excluded code outside the table (lower case included) raises `InvalidRequest`; so does an exclusion with no strictness. Codes are not normalised.
- `all_explicit` is true when every excluded allergen resolves to `FREE_FROM` or `CONTAINS`; a member `CONTAINS` counts as explicit for its family.
- GS1 names are the Name column of GS1 code list T4078 on gs1.se, read on 14 Nov through a page-to-text tool (3 public requests, nothing of ours sent). The 8 names our docs quote match; the other 37 deserve a spot check. The short labels are ours.
