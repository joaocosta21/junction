## Assumptions
- The `OfferBuilder` port takes an identity, the task a profile: the service resolves the profile; the three functions take the profile and the dependencies.
- An offer's clock is the `timeNow` of its availability answer. With no candidate there is no call and the clock port is read.
- A line with `product_ids` uses those products as given; its `product_type`, `name_terms` and `required_claims` do not filter them, the constraint checks report them. An unknown or deleted id fails the whole request with `unknown-product`.
- A search is cut at 1,000 candidates per line; a note says so.
- One exclusion reason per product and request line, the first of: `ALLERGEN_DECLARED`, `PORTION_PACK`, `PACK_SIZE_UNKNOWN`, the availability state, `LATE_FOR_NEEDED_BY`, `PRICE_UNAVAILABLE`, `ALLERGEN_NOT_STATED`. What does not match the request comes before what Valio cannot supply now.
- A `PORTION` line excludes other packs with reason `PORTION_PACK`; the sentence says which way.
- Allergen strictness is the stricter of profile and request line.
- Under confirmed-only, a product left out for a quantity or delivery date that is not confirmed has no reason code: it is named in `Offer.notes`, not in `excluded[]`.
- The lot step is read from the catalog port by rounding 0.001 up. Where the API sells another unit than the product file, the step is still the file's.
- A single product is chosen on the cost of the whole quantity; a combination is ordered by price per litre or kilogram, ties by id. Stock off the lot step is rounded down. `covers` never exceeds what was still missing.
- A request with several lines gives options that span all lines. Lines are filled in order and stock one line takes is not offered to the next. `fill_ratio` is the lowest.
- `ALTERNATIVE_UNCONFIRMED` exists whenever a fitting product is in that state, also beside a full confirmed fill. Its quantity is the one asked for.
- `SUBSTITUTE` is built when confirmed stock of the named products leaves something missing, also when the named product is only "quantity not confirmed" or past its cut-off (question 2). It keeps the confirmed lines of the named product and adds one line that fills the gap. One original per request line. It costs a second price and availability call.
- A stand-in in an offer carries the line's `required_claims`, obeys its pack rule and allergen exclusions, and under confirmed-only is fully confirmed.
- Option rank: an option holding a quantity that is not confirmed comes after the others; then fewer `NOT_MET` checks; then fewer `UNVERIFIED`; then `BEST_CONFIRMED`, `ALTERNATIVE_UNCONFIRMED`, `SUBSTITUTE` (question 3).
- Totals are sums of the rounded lines. Tax is net × `taxRate` per line; without a rate, the difference of Valio's two prices.
- A request line with nothing offered gets `NOT_MET` for everything it asked. A `PACK` check exists only when the pack is not `ANY`.
- Substitutes: `passes` is about the two allergen rules of §6 only. `orderable` adds price and availability. `reason` is set whenever the candidate cannot be ordered.
- Substitute candidates: the listed pairs plus the three category tiers, 999 at most, in one call each. Shown: every listed one; tiers are added until 3 can be ordered; 10 unlisted at most, those that can be ordered first.
- "Verification status" in the substitute ranking: can be ordered, then gating claims not confirmed, then how much of the quantity the stock leaves uncovered. Price is compared per litre or kilogram when both sizes are known. `price_difference` is per sales unit, as the contract says.
- Category distance 3 for a listed substitute that shares fewer than three digits (4 of 249 pairs, catalog).
- `find_substitutes` applies the profile's exclusions, no pack rule and no confirmed-only filter.
- `revalidate`: any price difference, a new price validity, a new state and a new cut-off are changes. Stock is a change only when it no longer covers what the option orders of that product (question 4). A cut-off or validity equal to now has passed, as in T04; the policy's F-04 expires strictly after.
