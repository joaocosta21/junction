## Assumptions
**`verify` does not take the signature in the task file.** The task says `verify(line, price, product, request_line, allergen_outcome, now)`. `RequestLine` has no `needed_by` (it is on `PurchaseRequest`) and its quantity is in litres or kg, not sales units, so the table cannot be evaluated from it. `verify` takes `quantity` (sales units of the offer line, `None` when no quantity was asked) and `needed_by` instead; everything after `product` is keyword-only. `allergen_outcome` is the `AllergenCheck` of T03 or `None`.

States
- `BLOCKED` when `salesBlock` is true on the product or on its schedule line (API.md §8.3).
- An amount of 0 or below is `OUT_OF_STOCK`. Only 0 is observed.
- **A cut-off exactly equal to now counts as passed** (`closing_time <= now`). A price whose `validTo` equals now is likewise no longer valid.
- A schedule line with no `closingTime` is not called `CUTOFF_PASSED`: it goes on to the quantity states, and its `delivery_date` claim is `UNKNOWN`. Not observed: every one of the 1,446 recorded lines with a schedule line carries a cut-off.
- `QUANTITY_NOT_CONFIRMED` at `availableAmount >= 999_999`, using the contract's `QUANTITY_NOT_CONFIRMED_THRESHOLD`; no second constant.
- `usable_amount` is `None` for `CUTOFF_PASSED` too, even when Valio shows a real figure (67 such Stock lines in Friday's sample).
- `is_late` looks at the dates only, whatever the state. The blocked product `470094` (2026-12-30) is late and blocked; the caller applies the state first. No orderable recorded line is late for 16 Nov.

Cells the SPEC §4 table leaves open — our reading
- `quantity`, cut-off passed → `UNKNOWN` (the figure belongs to a delivery that can no longer be ordered).
- `quantity`, state `CONFIRMED` but stock below the line's quantity → `RULED_OUT`, with both numbers.
- `quantity`, state `CONFIRMED` and no quantity asked → `CONFIRMED`.
- `delivery_date`, blocked / not deliverable / out of stock → `RULED_OUT`.
- `delivery_date`, cut-off passed → `RULED_OUT` even when no `needed_by` was given (the date Valio states cannot be met).
- `delivery_date`, Valio gives no cut-off or no date → `UNKNOWN`.
- `price`, `validTo` passed → `UNKNOWN` (the table has no `RULED_OUT` for price). "A net price" is `catalogPrice.value` on a line without `error`. `validFrom` is not checked: the table names `validTo` only, and no recorded price starts in the future.
- `allergens`, buyer excluded nothing (`None`, or a passing check with no finding, which is what T03 returns for no exclusions) → `CONFIRMED`, "The buyer excluded no allergen, so there is nothing to check."
- `allergens` otherwise reads T03's result and does not re-derive the rule: any `REJECT` → `RULED_OUT`; `all_explicit` and `PASS` → `CONFIRMED`; everything else → `UNKNOWN`. Sentences name the GS1 codes, not the allergen names.
- `origin`: the placeholders `zy`, `xs`, `zz` (PRODUCT-DATA.md) count as not stated. A numeric `countries_of_origin` list counts as stated when the two-letter code is missing or a placeholder (catalog: 124 products).

Wording
- Dates and times are written in the offset Valio sent (Helsinki), without a year for cut-off and delivery; price validity names the year and the time.
- Sales-unit codes (`ST`, `KI`, `KG` …) are shown as Valio sends them; no translation is published.
