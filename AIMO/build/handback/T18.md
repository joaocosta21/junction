## Assumptions
- The first bytes decide the media type sent to Gemini. The type the sender declares is passed along but never trusted or checked against them.
- AVIF is refused: it names the HEIF brand `mif1`, but Gemini's list does not name it.
- 5 MB means 5 × 1024 × 1024 bytes. A photo route's body may be that plus 64 KB.
- One photo is counted as one model call in the rate limit, although it makes two Gemini requests (transcription, then reading).
- Media resolution is left at the default (1,120 tokens an image for Gemini 3, the same as HIGH). The API stays on `v1`.
- The transcription leaves out crossed-out writing, writes `[?]` for an unreadable word and never guesses a number (`TRANSCRIBER_INSTRUCTION`).
- The lines are joined with line breaks into the request's `text`. `text_key` makes a recording match whatever the spacing.
- A photo on the page is read by the request parser and then offer → order of the top option (path 2 of `run.py`). It is not handed to the buyer agent, so the lines are read once (FIX-10 finding 7).
- "Never written to a log" means the application log. The lines become the request's `text`, which the offer and the "request received" event keep, as for typed text. A photo that cannot be read is recorded by its declared type and size only.
- The Finnish short forms `to` (Thursday) and `la` (Saturday) are not read as days, because they are English and French words ("allergic to", "à la carte"). A model's reading of them as a date is refused by the text check.
- No Finnish number words ("tuhat"): the task says Finnish numbers come as digits.
- A transcription of a photo is a recording like the others: served only for the profile it was recorded for.
- With Gemini down, a photo falls back only when both its transcription and its reading are recorded. It is then served wholly from them.
- The answer of `POST /v1/requests/read-image` is the API-layer model `ImageReading` (like `Health` in `routes.py`), not a contract. It documents itself in OpenAPI as `{"transcription", "request", "reading"}`.

## Contract changes needed
- `RequestParser.read_image(image, media_type, identity) -> tuple[list[str], Reading]`: the service calls it through `getattr`.
- `GatewayService.read_image(identity, image, media_type) -> tuple[tuple[str, ...], Reading]`: not on the port.
- `Reading`: no field says who transcribed a photo. Only `sentence` says it. Suggested: `Reading.transcribed_by: ReadingSource | None` plus `transcription: tuple[str, ...]`, which would also let the API answer be a contract.
- `ImageReading` (in `tta.api.uploads`): move it to `tta.contracts.agent` if the orchestrator wants it in `GET /v1/schemas`.
